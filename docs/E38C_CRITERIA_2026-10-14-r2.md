## Visual verifiability of task_done positives

A terminal-COMPLETE annotation does not guarantee the final screenshot itself
shows the goal achieved. Rule:

- Every positive (final screenshot) is reviewed visually with the question
  "can the goal's completion be verified from this screenshot ALONE?"
  Answers: `yes` / `no` / `doubtful`. Only pairs whose positive is `yes` are
  kept. The negative of a kept episode follows the r2 rule unchanged.
- Kept rows carry `label_origin = "annotation+visual-verification"` (the
  label now includes human judgment; declared).
- The coordinator independently reviews a subsample of 50 positives chosen by
  ascending sha256 over the kept positives; agreement rate and Cohen's kappa
  are reported. If agreement < 85 %, the real task_done family is reported in
  E39 as evaluation with label noise, without transfer claims.
- To keep >= 400 pairs after filtering, the sampled episode pool is extended
  up to ~ 800 COMPLETE test episodes (same selection rule), always inside the
  5 GB download budget.

The negative audit of 100 (fixed in the base criteria) proceeds unchanged;
if an audited negative's pair is dropped by the positive filter, the audit
observation still counts as measured.
