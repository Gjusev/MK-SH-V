# E38c criteria (FROZEN 2026-10-14)

E38c builds the **real-pixel evaluation floor** for E39: real permissive rows
turned into contract questions whose labels are derived by rule from the
original ground truth. Per the council verdict, real data is **evaluation
only** in E39 — no real row enters training before a later frozen experiment.

Frozen before any download of new data. The r1/r2 probes
(`docs/E38C_PROBE_CRITERIA_2026-10-14.md`, `-r2.md`) measured mappability
(CORD 94 %, GUI-Odyssey pairs 100 % rule-clean, operator review 19/20 valid
negatives, coordinator-agreed). After freezing, sources, counts, label rules,
audit rules and thresholds do not change; anything else needs an `-r2` file.

No training, no Kaggle. Total download budget: **≤ 5 GB**.

## Sources and families (frozen)

| Source | Hub repo | Data used | Family | Cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CORD v2 | `naver-clova-ix/cord-v2` | `validation` + `test` splits (200 receipts) | `field_threshold` (fixed class `receipt`, no discrimination claimed) | 1 per clean receipt × 3 languages |
| GUI-Odyssey | `OpenGVLab/GUI-Odyssey` | ~ 500 episodes with final action COMPLETE from the official **`task_split`/`test`** membership (chosen over random/device/app variants because test meta-tasks differ from train, matching the template-disjoint philosophy; choice frozen) | `task_done` | 2 per episode (final=`true`, early=`false`) × 3 languages |

Downloads: `splits/task_split.json` (~0.3 MB), episode screenshots by
individual `resolve` requests (~2 per episode), CORD rows via streaming
parquet. The monolithic `annotations.zip` is **not needed** (per-episode
annotation JSONs and split files are individually served); it is therefore
not downloaded at any size. All contract rows get `split="test"` (evaluation
floor); episodes are disjoint from anything else by construction.

## Label rules (frozen)

- **CORD**: the total is parsed from the row's own ground-truth JSON
  (`gt_parse.total.*_price`, exactly one; price parse rule as frozen in the
  r1 probe). The threshold is drawn **after** the total, around it:
  `thr = total × (1 ± δ)`, sign 50/50, δ log-uniform in [0.001, 0.5], rounded
  to a whole IDR amount; label = `total ≥ thr` (equality `true`), balanced
  50/50 by construction. Rejection reasons as in the r1 probe. The question
  names the threshold with dot-thousands formatting (the receipts' printed
  convention) and currency IDR, in EN/ES/DE with the E38a question template
  (declared deviation from E38a's per-language number formats: the corpus is
  Indonesian and the printed format is constant across languages).
- **GUI-Odyssey**: negative = screenshot of step
  `k = sha256(episode_id) % floor(n/2)` (r2 rule, unchanged); positive =
  final screenshot; label `true`/`false`; `group_id = episode_id`; both
  screenshots must differ. Questions use the E38b task_done template in
  EN/ES/DE; the goal text is the episode's `task` field in its source
  language (declared limitation: goal not translated).

## Declared label noise and post-build audit (frozen)

The negative class carries an estimated ~5 % label noise (1/20 operator
review; upper bound weak). Therefore, after construction: 100 negatives are
chosen by ascending sha256 over the final set's negative case ids and
reviewed visually by the probe operator; the coordinator independently
reviews a 20-case subsample for agreement. The reported numbers are the
noise rate and its 95 % Clopper-Pearson upper bound. **Fixed now**: if the
CP upper bound exceeds 15 %, the real task_done family is reported in E39 as
"evaluation with label noise" and no transfer claims are made on it.

## Declared limitation (frozen)

Negatives are early-episode screens, positives are final screens. A model
can distinguish "early vs final" without understanding the goal. E39 reports
this limitation with the task_done family results.

## Verifier gate (frozen)

`scripts/verify_e38c.py` (no generator import): (a) re-derives every label
from the original ground truth (CORD gt JSON; GUI-Odyssey steps annotation)
with its own parser and compares to the row; (b) checks every stored file's
SHA-256 against the manifest and every row against the contract; (c) checks
episode/group disjunction (one split per group; test-only); (d) G2 text-only
naive Bayes with positive control, adapted to an eval-only set: the NB is
trained on the corresponding **synthetic** family rows (E38a field_threshold
train for CORD; E38b task_done train for GUI-Odyssey) and evaluated on the
real cases — bar: accuracy ≤ majority + 0.05, positive control must saturate.

PASS iff all checks hold. Then, and only then, the dataset is uploaded to the
private Hub repo `Gjusev/MK-SH-V-E38c` (upload GO already given for private
repos; privacy re-verified by API after upload).

## Outputs

`data/e38c/` (git-ignored): `cord/` and `gui_odyssey/` rows.jsonl, images,
`manifest.json` with SHA-256 of every stored file; audit files under
`runs/e38c-audit/`; verifier report `runs/e38c-verify/verification.json`.
