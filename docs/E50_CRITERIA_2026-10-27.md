# E50 criteria (FROZEN 2026-10-27, before any E50 row is generated or measured)

## Question

E49 (result dd8dc39) raised goal grounding on fresh episodes of the task_split
**train** pool (M3F swap 0.830 / 0.843, own 0.893 / 0.863), but on the real
E38c set, whose episodes come from the task_split **test** pool, finished
screens with their own goal lost 6 to 8 pp. The reading "goal training makes
the model refuse unseen task types" was drawn from E38c, a set read many
times and not built for goal swaps.

E50 measures it directly, with no training: **on episodes of task types the
model has never seen, does E49 reject a mismatched goal, and does it still
accept the right one?** The answer decides what E51 trains.

## Data (generated by `scripts/gen_e50.py` after this commit)

- Source: GUI-Odyssey task_split **test**, COMPLETE episodes, the E41
  candidate procedure (stream, keep episodes whose last action is COMPLETE,
  sort by the same sha256 key). Excluded and asserted: every E38c episode,
  and (as a guard) every E41, E43 and E49 episode, none of which can be in
  the test split.
- Only the final screenshot of each episode is fetched (original bytes). The
  first 400 episodes in sorted order with a fetchable final screenshot form
  **M3T**.
- **M3T rows:** for each episode, the final screenshot with its own goal
  (gold true) and with the next different goal in M3T order (gold false), EN,
  the E43 swap rule; 800 rows. Never trained on, never used for selection,
  read once per checkpoint.

## Measurements

Per checkpoint: M3T swap accuracy, M3T own accuracy, episodes right both
ways, swap accuracy without swapped goals containing "any" (descriptive, as
in E49), and the mean p(true) on own and on swap rows.

## Readings fixed here (they choose E51; no release is at stake)

With Δ = E49 minus E47, per seed:

1. **Transfer**: E49 M3T swap ≥ 0.80 on both seeds.
2. **Own-goal cost on unseen tasks**: E49 M3T own ≥ E47 M3T own − 3 pp on
   both seeds.

- 1 and 2 hold: the E38c drop is not about goals on unseen tasks. E51 keeps
  the E49 recipe and looks for the GUI regression elsewhere.
- 1 holds, 2 fails: the goal gain transfers but costs own-goal recall on
  unseen tasks. E51 targets recall on unseen tasks (selection on a disjoint
  test-pool dev set, more own-goal positives), gated on a fresh test-pool set.
- 1 fails: the E49 gain does not transfer to unseen task types. E51 must
  change the goal data itself (task diversity), not the selection.

Everything is reported as measured; no checkpoint, threshold or rule is
changed after reading M3T.

## Result (2026-10-27, recorded after measurement; rules above unchanged)

Implemented by the implementer session (gen 023e53a, report a08c6da; 93 fast
tests pass). M3T rows sha in data/e50/manifest.json; 4 runs x 800 rows,
checkpoints sha-verified; report runs/e50/e50_report.json. Numbers reproduce
from the per-case files (reviewer recomputation matched).

| | E47 s42 | E47 s43 | E49 s42 | E49 s43 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3T swap (reject wrong goal) | 0.920 | 0.938 | 0.910 | 0.950 |
| M3T own (accept right goal) | 0.428 | 0.370 | 0.450 | 0.385 |
| episodes right both ways | 150 | 135 | 159 | 146 |
| mean p(true) own / swap | 0.43 / 0.09 | 0.38 / 0.08 | 0.46 / 0.10 | 0.39 / 0.07 |

Frozen readings: transfer PASSES (E49 swap 0.91/0.95 >= 0.80 both seeds, and
note E47 already at 0.92/0.94); own-cost PASSES (E49 own >= E47 - 3 pp, both
seeds). Outcome 1_and_2 as defined above.

Reviewer audit after the result (not a rule change, recorded as evidence): a
blind vision-model audit of the screens found the M3T own gold labels largely
unjustified. 6 of 6 sampled M3T own screenshots show an ordinary in-progress
screen (a ride-hail payment-selection step, plain Wikipedia articles, a video
page still loading), while 4 of 4 sampled E38c fin screenshots show the
goal's end artifact (notes fully written, shares delivered). E38c is 100 %
task_split test (verified), disjoint from M3T (verified). Readings under
this evidence:

- Wrong-goal rejection on unseen task types is 0.91-0.95 for BOTH E47 and
  E49. The released model already passes the 0.80 bar there; the historical
  0.78 on M3 is a property of that set, not of unseen tasks.
- The 0.37-0.45 M3T own number is dominated by gold-label noise (screens
  that do not show completion), not by model failure: on E38c, whose fin
  gold is visually backed, the same models accept own goals on unseen tasks
  at 0.83-0.88. "Goal grounding collapses on unseen task types" is NOT
  supported.
- E49 goal training does not hurt unseen tasks (own +2 pp, swap +-1 pp on
  M3T). The E38c fin drop measured in E49 (-6/-8 pp) reads as mild
  over-strictness on artifact screens, not an unseen-task effect.

Consequences: no E51 "goal rescue" experiment is warranted by this data. The
goal claim for the public model stays as published (end-of-task screen
detection; swap rejection is strong on unseen task types but was never
claimed). Remaining true gaps: mild strictness cost after goal training, and
real-image abstention. Also recorded: final-screenshot gold labels in
GUI-Odyssey are unreliable for completion judgments; any future goal set
must audit its gold visually (the blind vision audit used here is the
template).
