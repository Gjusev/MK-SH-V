# E50 criteria (FROZEN 2026-10-27, before any E50 row is generated or measured)

## Question

E49 (result dd8dc39) raised goal grounding on fresh episodes of the task_split
**train** pool (M3F swap 0.830 / 0.843, own 0.893 / 0.863), but on the real
E38c set, whose episodes come from the task_split **test** pool, finished
screens with their own goal lost 6 to 8 pp. The reading "goal training makes
the model refuse unseen task types" was drawn from E38c, a set read many
times and not built for goal swaps.

E50 measures it directly, with no training: **on episodes of task types the
model has never seen, does E49 reject a mismatched goal, and does it still
accept the right one?** The answer decides what E51 trains.

## Data (generated by `scripts/gen_e50.py` after this commit)

- Source: GUI-Odyssey task_split **test**, COMPLETE episodes, the E41
  candidate procedure (stream, keep episodes whose last action is COMPLETE,
  sort by the same sha256 key). Excluded and asserted: every E38c episode,
  and (as a guard) every E41, E43 and E49 episode, none of which can be in
  the test split.
- Only the final screenshot of each episode is fetched (original bytes). The
  first 400 episodes in sorted order with a fetchable final screenshot form
  **M3T**.
- **M3T rows:** for each episode, the final screenshot with its own goal
  (gold true) and with the next different goal in M3T order (gold false), EN,
  the E43 swap rule; 800 rows. Never trained on, never used for selection,
  read once per checkpoint.

## Measurements

Per checkpoint: M3T swap accuracy, M3T own accuracy, episodes right both
ways, swap accuracy without swapped goals containing "any" (descriptive, as
in E49), and the mean p(true) on own and on swap rows.

## Readings fixed here (they choose E51; no release is at stake)

With Δ = E49 minus E47, per seed:

1. **Transfer**: E49 M3T swap ≥ 0.80 on both seeds.
2. **Own-goal cost on unseen tasks**: E49 M3T own ≥ E47 M3T own − 3 pp on
   both seeds.

- 1 and 2 hold: the E38c drop is not about goals on unseen tasks. E51 keeps
  the E49 recipe and looks for the GUI regression elsewhere.
- 1 holds, 2 fails: the goal gain transfers but costs own-goal recall on
  unseen tasks. E51 targets recall on unseen tasks (selection on a disjoint
  test-pool dev set, more own-goal positives), gated on a fresh test-pool set.
- 1 fails: the E49 gain does not transfer to unseen task types. E51 must
  change the goal data itself (task diversity), not the selection.

Everything is reported as measured; no checkpoint, threshold or rule is
changed after reading M3T.
