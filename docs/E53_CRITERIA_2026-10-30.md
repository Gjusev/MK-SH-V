## Gates (fail any → the e52 checkpoints are not releasable; no post-hoc changes)

- **R1 (gate claimable)**: on synthetic test non-FR at each seed's fitted
  (T, tau): CP95 upper ≤ 0.05 AND coverage ≥ 0.90.
- **R2 (no silent drift)**: full accuracy on synthetic test non-FR ≥ 0.9488
  (the released s43's 0.9588 − 1.0 pp) for the seed being considered for
  release.
- **R3 (recommendation rule, pre-registered)**: recommend replacing the
  released s43 with e52-s43 iff R1 and R2 hold for e52-s43 AND its all-real
  accuracy (GUI + CORD, 2,586) ≥ 0.807 (the released number). The decision
  stays the user's.
