# E40 criteria (FROZEN 2026-10-21)

E40 answers one question: **does the E39 finalist (A1-V) deliver calibrated
probabilities and a validated abstention — selective risk held at/below the
5 % budget on held-out sets, with Clopper-Pearson bounds per set?** No model
is retrained; the frozen E39 checkpoints are evaluated. E40 runs AFTER E39's
result (GO-E40 with limitations), on data the training never touched.

- Checkpoints (best.pt sha256, in-kernel verified, from the private datasets

- Data: the private Kaggle datasets `gjusev/mkshv-e38a`, `-e38b`, `-e38c`

## Procedure (identical per seed 42/43/44; no cross-seed pooling)

1. **Evaluate probabilities** on `cal_temperature`, `cal_policy`, test non-FR,
   test FR, and the real set (one forward per case; full option vectors).
2. **Temperature T**: single scalar per checkpoint, fitted on
   `cal_temperature` by minimising NLL (grid 0.50–3.00 step 0.01, then local
   refinement ±0.01 step 0.001) — `T*` frozen before any held-out table is
   computed. Ablation (descriptive, reported, never used for claims):
   `T_doc` fitted on the 3 document families only, `T_screen` on the 4 screen
   families only.
3. **Threshold τ**: the MK-SH-1 rule on `cal_policy` with T-scaled
   probabilities — grid 0.30–0.95 step 0.05; τ* = the SMALLEST grid value
   whose selective risk (error rate among accepted, argmax≠gold) is ≤ 0.05
   on the whole split; if none reaches ≤ 0.05, τ* = 0.95 and the miss is
   reported (abstention budget not achievable at this checkpoint).
4. **Held-out tables** (per seed, T* and τ* applied; no re-tuning): for each
   set — test non-FR (overall + per family), test FR, CORD, GUI-Odyssey, and
   the E35 text sets testC/task (descriptive): coverage, empirical selective
   risk, and the Clopper-Pearson 95 % UPPER bound of selective risk among
   accepted; full risk–coverage curves per family and set over the τ grid.
5. **Claim rule (fixed now):** a set "meets the abstention budget" for a seed
   iff the CP95 upper bound of its selective risk is ≤ 0.05 at τ*. Claims in
   the report and model card are limited to sets that meet the budget for
   ALL three seeds; sets that do not are reported with their bounds and
   carry no claim.

## Gate / outcome

- E39's G1–G3 conclusions are untouched. E40's outcome is the complete table
  (all sets × 3 seeds + curves + bounds) and the per-set budget flags; there
  is no additional go/no-go binary. Any missing piece is `not_run` with a
  reason; failures of descriptive ablations never void the main table.
- Everything runs in ONE kernel (`gjusev/mkshv-e40-calib`), one shot, no
  retries that touch numbers.
