MK-SH-V is out: a 260M model that reads document pages and screenshots and answers typed questions with calibrated probabilities, a choice, or an explicit refusal.

What we measured, on held-out data:
- Real screenshots ("is the goal done?"): 0.826, against 0.731 for a ~15x larger model run zero-shot on the same 2,010 pairs.
- The refusal gate (tau 0.55): 86 % coverage with a 95 % Clopper-Pearson upper bound on selective risk of 3.2 to 3.4 % on our synthetic test, both seeds.
- Where it stops: on real receipts and real screenshots the gate is not yet validated, and on receipt thresholds the larger model and a rules baseline do as well or better. That is published next to the wins.

Criteria frozen before every measurement, every number regenerable from per-case files.
