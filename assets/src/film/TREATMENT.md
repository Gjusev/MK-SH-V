# Treatment

Logline: a small model that answers when it can see, and refuses when it can't.

Specificity test: a simpler model would answer every question with a
probability. This one carries a gate whose risk bound was validated held-out,
and the film shows it refusing a real receipt where its top pick would have
been wrong. That is the part a simpler product cannot show.

Device: the amber scan line (the gate's colour, the band of the mark). It sweeps the
screenshot, becomes the tau tick on the probability bars, becomes the 5 %
budget line on the risk meter, and resolves into the band under the mark.

Draw (pick.py seed 421416044, launch, high, 25 s), placement:
